| 1 | `health` (lines 72-74) | Returns `{"status": "ok"}` unconditionally without verifying database connectivity. | 2 | `create_employee` (lines 79-83) | Check-then-insert race condition: checks `find_one` before `insert_one`. ---

### Non-defects Analyzed and Retained

1. **Natural Key Addressing for Attendance Records (`(emp_code, date)`):**
   - *Observation:* Attendance records do not have an `id` field in the database model or API contract.
   - *Conclusion:* This is intentional design per section 5 of `DATA_MODEL.md` and OpenAPI specification; records are uniquely identified by composite natural key `(emp_code, date)`.

2. **Shift Times and Dates Stored as Plain Strings:**
   - *Observation:* `shift_start` and `shift_end` are stored as `"HH:MM"` and `date` as `"YYYY-MM-DD"`.
   - *Conclusion:* Intentionally kept as plain strings to prevent artificial time zones from distorting calendar dates and fixed shift boundaries (as detailed in `DATA_MODEL.md` section 1).

3. **Absence and Leave Omission from Creation Endpoints:**
   - *Observation:* No punch endpoint creates `ABSENT` or `LEAVE` records directly.
   - *Conclusion:* Intentional per business rule R6 / `DATA_MODEL.md`; absence and leave records are either seeded by HR back-office systems or generated via regularization (`PATCH`).
